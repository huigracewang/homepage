<strong style="color:#2b2b2b">Biography:</strong>

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at Bentley University. My research is in household finance and corporate finance, with a focus on how incentive frictions in financial markets shape the decisions of households, firms, and financial institutions. My recent work also examines how new technologies, such as industrial robots and generative AI, are changing the value of workers’ skills.

My research has been published in the <strong style="font-weight:700"><i>Journal of Financial Economics</i></strong> and <strong style="font-weight:700"><i>Management Science</i></strong>, and presented at the annual meetings of the American Finance Association, Western Finance Association, and European Finance Association, as well as the SFS Cavalcade. My work on student loans has been featured on the LSE USAPP Blog and in the American Economic Association’s poster program.

I joined Bentley University in 2020 after receiving my Ph.D. in Finance from the University of Texas at Dallas.
