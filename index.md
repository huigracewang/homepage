<strong style="color:#2b2b2b">Biography:</strong>

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at Bentley University. My research spans household and corporate finance, examining how financial frictions, incentives, and beliefs shape the decisions of households, financial analysts, firms, and financial institutions.

My research has been published in the <strong style="font-weight:700"><i>Journal of Financial Economics</i></strong> and <strong style="font-weight:700"><i>Management Science</i></strong>, and presented at the annual meetings of the American Finance Association, Western Finance Association, and European Finance Association, as well as the SFS Cavalcade.

I joined Bentley University in 2020 after receiving my Ph.D. in Finance from the University of Texas at Dallas.
